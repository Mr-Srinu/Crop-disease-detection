Classification Analytics

Technical evaluation of state-of-the-art CNN architectures trained on massive crop disease datasets, showcasing diagnostic precision across multiple pathogens.

Class Distribution

Illustrates the balance of infected and healthy leaf categories within the dataset. A uniform distribution is vital to ensure the CNN models learn to identify rare pathogens as effectively as common health states, preventing diagnostic bias.

Confusion Matrix & ROC Curve

Provides a detailed breakdown of correct and incorrect predictions across all classes. This visualization confirms the system's diagnostic confidence and its ability to distinguish between pathogens with similar visual characteristics.

Accuracy

Represents the overall percentage of correct identifications made by the system. High accuracy confirms that the deep learning architecture can reliably differentiate between healthy leaves and those affected by various pathogens.

Precision

Measures the system's ability to minimize false positive diagnoses. In agricultural diagnostics, high precision ensures that healthy leaves are not incorrectly flagged as diseased, reducing unnecessary chemical treatments.

Recall

Evaluates the model's effectiveness in capturing every instance of infectious disease. Superior recall is critical for early detection, ensuring that no early-stage pathogen infections are missed during field scans.

F1-Score

Acts as the harmonic mean of precision and recall, providing a balanced assessment of model robustness. This unified metric confirms that the diagnostic system is both precise and highly sensitive across all disease categories.
